FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for quotes?+

No — site visits and quotes are free across our service area. We'll measure up, talk through your ideas and send an itemised quote.

How long does a typical landscaping job take?+

A garden makeover or new turf can take a few days. A full backyard with paving, retaining walls and decking usually runs over a few weeks, depending on size, access and weather. We'll give you a timeline with your quote.

Will native plants look messy or bare?+

Not at all. Natives can be styled as neat and modern or soft and cottage-like. We layer grasses, shrubs and groundcovers so the garden looks full and stays green through summer.

Can you work with clay soil and drainage problems?+

Yes. Heavy clay is common across the Yarra Valley and Melbourne's east. We improve the soil, add drainage where needed and choose plants that cope with wet winters and dry summers.

Can you fix or install an automated watering system?+

Yes. We design and install drip and spray systems with timers and zones, and can sort out existing systems that aren't working properly — even the complicated ones.

What's the best time of year to start?+

Autumn and spring are ideal for planting in Melbourne, but hardscaping like paving, walls and decks can go ahead most of the year. Booking early in spring helps you get in before the summer rush.

Are you insured?+

Yes, we carry public liability insurance and are happy to provide details with your quote.
